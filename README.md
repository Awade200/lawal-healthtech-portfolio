## Selected work

### 1. Sulawog Clinic Management System
**HealthTech product and operations**

I helped translate the real clinic journey into a structured digital workflow covering registration, walk-ins, consultation, treatment, billing, payment and follow-up.

A key product decision came from observing actual use: the original flow leaned too heavily on appointments, while the clinic handled many walk-in patients. The workflow was adjusted around how the service really operated rather than forcing users into an appointment-first model.

**Product evidence:**
- workflow mapping
- patient-journey design
- requirements definition
- stakeholder coordination
- iteration from user behaviour
- billing and operational visibility

> The underlying operational system is private because it contains business and healthcare workflows. The public portfolio uses privacy-safe illustrations and case-study material.

### 2. Sulawog Operational Systems
**Inventory, remittance, expenses and management control**

I worked on systems designed to make stock, movements, customer balances, revenue, expenses and management reporting easier to track and act on.

The product challenge was not simply storing more information. It was creating a clearer operating view of what was available, what moved, what was sold, what remained outstanding and what management needed to do next.

**Product evidence:**
- operational requirements
- inventory workflows
- role-based information needs
- reporting structure
- traceable corrections and controls
- management dashboards

### 3. Recruitment Operations Platform
**Application and interview workflow**

I worked on a recruitment system that structured the journey from application through review, shortlisting, interview scheduling and recorded outcomes.

The focus was reducing manual coordination while giving administrators and interviewers a clearer view of candidate status and next actions.

**Product evidence:**
- status-based workflow design
- admin tooling
- interview scheduling flows
- stakeholder-specific views
- process automation
- operational reporting

### 4. Shift Manager
**Personal workflow product**

A public web application for variable-shift workers to organise schedules and turn shift information into a clearer operational view.

Repository: https://github.com/Awade200/shiftmanager

The product includes flows for adding and importing shifts, reviewing current and historical schedules, calendar views and analytics.
